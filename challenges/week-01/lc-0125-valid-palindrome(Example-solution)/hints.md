# Hints

## Hint 1

Ignore characters that are not letters or digits.

---

## Hint 2

Uppercase and lowercase letters should be treated as the same character.

---

## Hint 3

Use two pointers.

One pointer starts from the beginning, the other from the end.

---

## Hint 4

Move each pointer until it reaches an alphanumeric character.

Compare both characters after converting them to lowercase.

If they differ, the string is not a palindrome.
