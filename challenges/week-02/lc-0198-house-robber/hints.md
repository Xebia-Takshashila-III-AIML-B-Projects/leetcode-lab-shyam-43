# 💡 Hints - LC-0198: House Robber

Reveal one hint at a time.

---

# Hint 1

You cannot rob two adjacent houses.

---

# Hint 2

At every house you have only two choices.

- Rob it.
- Skip it.

---

# Hint 3

If you rob the current house,

you must skip the previous one.

---

# Hint 4

If you skip the current house,

you keep the best answer obtained so far.

---

# Hint 5

Maintain the best possible amount after processing each house.

---

# Final Hint

The answer for each house depends only on the previous two computed values.

This is a Dynamic Programming problem.

Good luck! 🚀
