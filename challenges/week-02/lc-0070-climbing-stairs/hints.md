# 💡 Hints - LC-0070: Climbing Stairs

Reveal one hint at a time.

---

# Hint 1

Think about small values first.

How many ways are there to climb

- 1 stair?
- 2 stairs?
- 3 stairs?

---

# Hint 2

To reach step `n`,

you must come from either

- step `n - 1`
- step `n - 2`

---

# Hint 3

Can you express the answer for step `n`

using the answers for previous steps?

---

# Hint 4

This problem follows a familiar mathematical sequence.

Can you identify it?

---

# Hint 5

Instead of using recursion,

can you build the answer iteratively?

---

# Final Hint

Maintain only the previous two answers while traversing from step 1 to step n.

Good luck! 🚀
